# Landing card illustration review

The supplied new reference replaces the right-side circular waveform with layered plan, progress, and material cards. The cards are a feature preview, so they contain no invented student results, dates, task history, or score.

## Before

- [Desktop](desktop-before.png)
- [Small desktop](small-desktop-before.png)
- [Mobile](mobile-before.png)

The circle did not communicate the study journey and sat behind mobile copy.

## First pass

- [Desktop](desktop-pass-1.png)
- [Short desktop](short-desktop-pass-1.png)
- [Small desktop](small-desktop-pass-1.png)
- [Mobile](mobile-pass-1.png)
- [Small mobile](small-mobile-pass-1.png)

The layered cards matched the reference direction, but the smaller desktop card clipped at the right edge. The miniature mobile stack could not be read and crowded the CTA on short screens.

## Second and third passes

- [Second pass at small desktop](small-desktop-pass-2.png)
- [Second pass on mobile](mobile-pass-2.png)
- [Third pass at narrow desktop](narrow-desktop-pass-3.png)
- [Third pass on mobile](mobile-pass-3.png)

The collage moved inside the viewport, progress graphics changed to feature labels, and mobile gained a single readable plan card. A final adjustment reduced overlap between the plan and progress cards.

## Final

- [Desktop](desktop-after.png)
- [Short desktop](short-desktop-after.png)
- [Small desktop](small-desktop-after.png)
- [Narrow desktop](narrow-desktop-after.png)
- [Tall mobile](mobile-after.png)

Across these widths, the page and CTA fit without scrolling. The progress card stays within the viewport and does not cover the plan text. The tall-phone card clears the CTA; short phones hide the decoration. The desktop card moves gently by about 6 px, and the animation is disabled when reduced motion is requested.
