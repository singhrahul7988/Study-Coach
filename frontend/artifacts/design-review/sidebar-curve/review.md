# Sidebar curve review

## Before

- [Expanded desktop](expanded-before.png)
- [Collapsed desktop](collapsed-before.png)

The full-height edge border and brand divider made a rigid cross at the top. The collapse control added a third outlined edge at the bottom.

## After

- [Expanded desktop, 1280 px](expanded-1280-after.png)
- [Collapsed desktop, 1280 px](collapsed-1280-after.png)
- [Expanded desktop, 1440 px](expanded-1440-after.png)
- [Collapsed desktop, 1440 px](collapsed-1440-after.png)
- [Mobile](mobile-after.png)

The sidebar now sits as a rounded white rail on a pale surface. A soft shadow separates it from content, while the topbar uses a subtle lower-left curve and shadow instead of a hard underline. The bottom collapse control uses the same rounded blue treatment as navigation.

Measured in both desktop widths: logo and nav icon centers are x=39.5 px; brand and nav text start at x=67 px; brand and topbar centers are y=42 px. Collapsed logo, nav icons, and toggle all center at x=39 px. The mobile sidebar remains hidden and the page has no horizontal overflow.
