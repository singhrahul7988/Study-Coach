# Badge visual review

Reference: the supplied Milestones & badges screenshot with vivid hexagonal emblems, subtle card color, compact status pills, and progress bars.

## Before

- [Desktop badge panel](desktop-before.png)
- [Mobile badge panel](mobile-before.png)

The prior row had plain square icons, large neutral cards, and weak distinction between earned, in-progress, and locked achievements.

## Iteration 1

- [Desktop](desktop-pass-1.png)
- [Mobile](mobile-pass-1.png)

The layered hexagonal emblems and status treatments matched the reference direction. Six desktop cards compressed descriptions to 10 px in the available profile width, while the mobile card had excess height.

## Refined result

- [Desktop badge panel](desktop-after.png)
- [Mobile badge panel](mobile-after.png)
- [New-student preview](desktop-empty-after.png)
- [Full desktop profile](profile-desktop-after.png)

Five cards now fit at common desktop widths, with larger emblems and more readable titles and descriptions. The mobile card is shorter. Carousel controls retain access to the remaining achievements. Earned, progress, and locked colors reflect recorded task and study-day state; no dates, marks, or accuracy figures are invented.
